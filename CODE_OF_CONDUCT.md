# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Rustify project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, level of
experience, or technical choices.

## Our Standards

Be respectful, assume good faith, stay on topic, and keep criticism technical
and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, and posting others' private
information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this
Code of Conduct. Reports can be made to the maintainers through GitHub contact
information; reports are handled confidentially.

Instances of abusive behaviour may be reported to the project team, which will
review and take appropriate action.
